How to read · definitions · sources

Bars above zero are originally wrong images that become correct; bars below zero are originally correct images that become wrong. Dark orange identifies new errors with confidence ≥90%. The black line is recovered minus new errors, so it measures the net accuracy change. All contributions use the same full-test denominator, not different correct/wrong group sizes; switches between two wrong labels do not contribute. Header percentages show accuracy at the selected level, with the change from the original prediction beside them; hover for the original accuracy.

Zoom is linked across all three models. Drag a rectangle or use the wheel to zoom, click a bar or move the slider to inspect a level, use Fit for the current transformation’s range, or Reset / double-click for the full range. Changing transformations resets zoom. Noise counts are means of three realizations, not independent extra images. Transformed inputs may leave the data distribution or change the label: these views describe checkpoint responses, not a correction strategy.
