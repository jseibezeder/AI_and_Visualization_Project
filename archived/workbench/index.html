Full-TEST native reliability context (cached, unedited) and workbench limits
Confidence/error/reliability on a selected edited cohort is descriptive, not independent calibration validation. Original labels may no longer apply after editing. Automatic masks are generated on the original image and must be visually inspected; they can miss pale cytoplasm or include neighboring material. ROI area and perturbation RMS are unequal, and effects need not add linearly. PCA moves preserve the pixel residual but clip to valid RGB; they do not validate global 2D boundaries. Small class strata can produce degenerate bootstrap intervals; no multiple-testing correction is performed. Use notebook population results for context, not a handful of chosen examples as prevalence estimates.
